How to Play

1

Choose a game mode — One vs One or Player vs Computer.

2

Pick your symbol, X or O. X always moves first.

3

Players take turns placing their symbol on an empty cell.

4

Get three matching symbols in a row to win — horizontal, vertical, or diagonal.

5

If all nine cells fill up with no winner, the game ends in a draw.

6

Stuck? Tap Hint during a game to see a suggested move highlighted on the board.
